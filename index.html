Dressed to Kill

  1. Dress the part

    Send the invitations, then each guest's character. They arrive in costume as the staff and regulars of a 1926 speakeasy, each with a secret to keep.

  2. Check the alibis

    Over cocktails, dinner and dessert, the clue cards and evidence come out a round at a time. Everyone says where they were, and when. Someone's alibi is running out of time.

    After dessert, everyone writes down a name in silence. Then you read the solution aloud, and the room finds out who kept a straight face.

Hosting

Questions Before the Questioning

How many people can play?

4 to 12 guests, plus you as host. Four core characters are always cast, and up to eight optional ones join as the guest list grows. No rewrites.

Does the host play?

You run the night and you know the solution, so you don't play one of the four core suspects. Short a guest? You can take an optional part. Those carry nothing essential, so you can't give the game away.

How long does it take?

About three hours, with dinner in three stages: cocktails, the main course, dessert. With four guests it runs nearer two and a half.

How much prep is there?

Send the invitations about three weeks ahead and each guest's booklet about ten days ahead. The day before, print and cut out the clue cards and print the evidence, on plain paper at home. Card stock is nice, never needed.

What if a guest cancels?

An optional character simply drops out, cards and all. If a core player cancels, the host guide shows you how to recast or read their part.

What paper does it print on?

US Letter and A4, in colour or black and white. The covers, invitations, menus and door sign also come in a deluxe black-and-gold edition, and every character booklet comes in a version to read on a phone.

Does anything ship?

No. It's a digital download: the files are yours as soon as you buy, and you print what you need. Nothing arrives in the post.

What if something goes wrong?

If a file won't open, prints wrong or something is missing, we fix it the same day or refund you. Downloaded files can't be returned for a change of mind. The details are on the refunds page.
